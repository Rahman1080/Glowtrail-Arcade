GLOWTRAIL Arcade

Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 17, 2026

1. Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how GLOWTRAIL Arcade ("the App", "we", "our", or "us") handles information when you use the application.

GLOWTRAIL Arcade is designed as a local, single-player arcade application. We currently do not require users to create an account or provide personal information to play.

2. Information We Collect

We currently do not directly collect personal information from users.

The App does not currently require you to provide your name, email address, phone number, postal address, account credentials, or other directly identifying information.

3. Game Progress and Local Storage

The App stores game-related information locally on your device so that your progress can persist between sessions.

This may include information such as:

  • Game progress and completed levels
  • Scores and best scores
  • Stars and game results
  • Daily challenge progress
  • Streak information
  • Game settings and preferences
  • Accessibility preferences
  • Unlocked cosmetic or progression information

This information is used to provide core game functionality, such as saving your progress, restoring settings, and maintaining game statistics.

GLOWTRAIL Arcade does not currently provide a user account or cloud synchronization system for this information.

4. Analytics

GLOWTRAIL Arcade currently includes an internal analytics interface in its software architecture for possible future use. At the current time, the application uses a no-operation analytics implementation and does not send gameplay analytics to an external analytics provider.

If this changes in a future version, this Privacy Policy will be updated to describe the information collected, the purpose of collection, the relevant service provider, and applicable choices available to users.

5. Advertising

GLOWTRAIL Arcade currently does not display third-party advertisements and does not currently use an advertising network SDK to serve ads.

If advertising is introduced in a future version, this Privacy Policy will be updated before or at the time the relevant functionality is introduced to explain applicable data collection, sharing, and advertising practices.

6. Internet and Network Access

The Android application declares Internet access as part of its application configuration. The current game architecture does not require users to submit personal information to use the core single-player gameplay features.

Network behavior may also be affected by the Android platform, application framework, hosting resources, or future services integrated into the App.

7. Information Sharing

We do not currently sell, rent, or share user-provided personal information with third parties because the App does not currently collect user-provided personal information as part of its core gameplay.

This policy will be updated if third-party services are added that process user or device information.

8. Data Retention and Deletion

Game progress and settings stored locally remain on the device until they are removed by the user, removed by the Android operating system, the App is uninstalled, or application data is otherwise cleared.

Because the current App does not provide a user account or cloud-based personal profile, we do not currently maintain a server-side personal account database for users of the App.

9. Children's Privacy

GLOWTRAIL Arcade does not knowingly request children to submit personal information such as names, email addresses, or phone numbers.

If the App's target audience, advertising systems, analytics, or data practices change, this policy will be reviewed and updated to reflect applicable child privacy requirements.

10. Security

We take reasonable steps to protect the information handled by the App. The App is primarily designed around local game data and does not currently require a user account containing personal information.

No method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the App's functionality, services, or data practices change.

When changes are made, the "Last updated" date at the top of this page will be updated.
